C1 grammar map

Structures that carry meaning in on-screen dialogue: the pattern, one line you might hear, and where to read more.

Certainty & necessity

StructurePatternOn screen
Past deductionmust / might / can't have + pp"You must have known." · "She can't have left." (not mustn't have)
Unnecessary past actionneedn't have + pp
didn't need to + inf		"I needn't have cooked" = I cooked; it was pointless.
"I didn't need to cook" = no necessity; maybe I didn't.

Inversion & emphasis

StructurePatternOn screen
Inverted conditionalsShould / Were / Had + subject…"Should you need anything…" · "Had I known, I'd never have come."
Negative adverbial inversionLittle / Never / No sooner + aux + subject"Little did he know…" · "No sooner had we sat down than…"
Cleft sentencesWhat X is… / It was X that…"What I can't stand is the lying." · "It was you who told her!"
Emphatic auxiliarydo / does / did (stressed) + inf"I did lock it!" · "She does care, you know."

Unreal time

StructurePatternOn screen
Past form = not real, not past time. Unreal now → past simple; unreal past → past perfect. Lesson 2
wish / if only+ past simple (now)
+ past perfect (past)
+ would (annoyance)		"I wish I knew." · "If only I'd listened." · "I wish you'd stop that!"
It's (high / about) time+ subject + past simple
no subject: + to-inf		"It's high time we went." · "It's time to go."
would rather+ other subject + past
same subject: + bare inf		"I'd rather you didn't." · "I'd rather stay in."
as if / as though+ past (unreal comparison)

Ellipsis in conversation

From Lesson 4.

StructurePatternOn screen example
So do Iso + auxiliary + subject (agree with a positive); use do/does/did if there's no auxiliary"I loved the finale." "So did I!"
Neither have Ineither / nor + auxiliary + subject (agree with a negative); casual: Me neither"I've never been to Scotland." "Neither have I."
I hope so / I hope notso / not replace a whole clause; never I don't hope so"Is it going to rain?" "I hope not."
I'm afraid so / notpolite bad news: unfortunately yes / no"Is she in?" "I'm afraid not."
I don't think sonegative with think / believe / expect; I think not is formal"Is Tom coming?" "I don't think so."
I'd love tokeep to, drop the repeated verb phrase"Fancy a drink?" "I'd love to."
Dropped subject[It / I] + verb, informal"Sounds good." · "Hope you have a nice time."
Dropped subject + auxiliary[Do you / I've] + verb, informal"Want a lift?" · "Seen it."
Dropped auxiliary[Are / Have] + you… in questions; not with I"You coming tonight?"

Present tenses for attitude

StructurePatternOn screen
Simple vs continuous can show attitude, not just time. Lesson 7
Annoying habitsalways / constantly / forever + present continuous"You're always taking my charger!" · "He's forever telling me what to do."
Temporary behaviourbe being + adjective"You're being ridiculous." · "Why is he being so nice?"
State verbs as actionslove / see / have + -ing = new meaning"I'm loving this." (enjoying now) · "She's seeing someone." (dating) · "We're having a meeting."
SofteningI'm hoping… · I was hoping / wondering… (most polite)"I was wondering if you could help." · "I was hoping you could lend me the car."
Narrative presentpresent simple for events, continuous for background"So this guy walks into a bar…" · "I'm standing there and she comes up to me…"

Reporting verbs

StructurePatternOn screen
The verb carries the attitude; each one has its own pattern. Lesson 8
Verb + -ingdeny / admit / suggest + -ing"He denied taking it." · "She suggested waiting."
Verb (+ person) + preposition + -ingaccuse sb of · blame sb for · apologise for · insist on · admit to"She accused me of lying!" · "He blames me for everything." · "She insisted on paying."
Verb (+ person) + to-infinitiveurge sb to · warn sb (not) to · threaten to (no person)"I warned you not to trust him." · "She threatened to call the police."
Verb + that-clauseinsist / suggest / admit / deny + (that)"He insists he did nothing wrong." · "I suggest you leave early." (not suggest you to leave)

Advanced passives

From Lesson 9.

StructurePatternOn screen example
Impersonal reporting passiveIt is said / thought / believed that + clause"It's thought that the fire was started deliberately."
Personal reporting passive (same time)subject + is said / thought / believed to + infinitive"She's said to be furious."
Personal reporting passive (earlier)subject + is said / believed to have + past participle"The suspect is believed to have fled the country."
Get-passiveget + past participle; informal, often unwanted events"He got sacked." (US usually got fired) · "We got stuck."
Have / get something done (service)have / get + object + past participle; get is less formal"I'm getting my hair cut on Saturday."
Have something done (bad experience)have + object + past participle; something happened to you"He had his car stolen."
Passive -ingbeing + past participle"I hate being told what to do."
