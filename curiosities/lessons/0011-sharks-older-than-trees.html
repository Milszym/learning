Sharks older than trees

Sharks were swimming long before the first forest grew.

Time ~2 min Win a deep-time fact with two clear numbers Bonus 1 C1 word

Guess firstWhich appeared first on Earth: sharks or trees? Decide before you scroll.

Sharks are older: shark-like scales date back about 450 million years, the first trees about 390 million.

~450myears ago: oldest shark-like scales
vs
~390myears ago: oldest known fossil forest

Why it feels wrong: Trees feel like permanent scenery, but early land plants were small and low. The oldest known fossil forest, on the Somerset coast, is about 390 million years old.2 Shark-like scales go back about 450 million years, though scientists debate whether they come from true sharks; even the oldest shark-like teeth, about 410 million years old, beat the trees.1
