A light-day away

Our farthest spacecraft is so far that even light needs a day.

Time ~2 min Win a sense of how far "far" really is Bonus 1 C1 word

Guess firstVoyager 1 left Earth in 1977 and is the most distant human-made object. How long does its radio signal, moving at the speed of light, take to reach us now? Decide before you scroll.

Its signal takes almost a full day, and on 18 November 2026 it takes exactly one.

Why it feels wrong: Light crosses from the Moon in about a second, so "a day" sounds impossible. But Voyager 1 has flown for 49 years.1 In mid-2026 its signal took about 23.6 hours;2 NASA calculates it crosses one light-day, 25.9 billion km, on 18 November 2026.1 The exact figure shifts slightly as Earth circles the Sun.

far-flungadjective

Used for places a great distance away, or for something spread over a very large area.3 Voyager 1 is our most far-flung messenger: its signal takes a day to get home.
