Lesson 7 · Grammar

Present tenses for attitude

Why "You're always doing that" is a complaint, and "I'm seeing someone" is gossip.

Time ~3 min Win hear the feeling behind 4 present-tense choices Mission catch irritation and politeness in dialogue

The big idea

At C1, I do vs I'm doing is often about attitude, not time: the continuous can sound irritated, temporary or softer.1

Annoying habits

always / constantly / forever + present continuous

You're always taking my charger!2

Behaviour right now

be being + adjective

You're being ridiculous. (how you're acting now)3

State verbs, used as actions

love / see / have… + -ing = a new meaning

Is she seeing anyone? (dating)4

Softening

I'm hoping… · I was hoping / wondering…

I was wondering if you could help.5
