Lesson 8 · Grammar

Reporting verbs

"She accused me of lying!" How characters retell a fight in one verb.

Time ~12 min Win use 10 reporting verbs with the right pattern Mission follow who blamed whom in novels and retold arguments

The big idea

Novels and characters rarely say she said that… for long. They choose a verb that carries the attitude: accuse, deny, admit, insist. Each verb comes with its own pattern, so learn them in groups.1

Verb + -ing

deny · admit · suggest + -ing

He denied taking the money.

She admitted making a mistake.3

Dora suggested waiting until Monday.

Verb (+ person) + preposition + -ing

accuse sb of · blame sb for · apologise for · insist on · admit to

She accused me of lying!4

He blames his mother for everything.

She insisted on paying. · He finally admitted to it.

Verb (+ person) + to-infinitive

urge sb to · warn sb (not) to · threaten to

My sister warned me not to trust him.5

They urged her to run for office.

She threatened to call the police. (no person before to: threatened us to call)6

Verb + that-clause

insist · suggest · admit · deny + (that)

Greg insists (that) he did nothing wrong.

I suggest (that) you leave around four.2

In speech, that is often dropped.

Polish trap

Zasugerował mi, żebym… tempts you to say He suggested me to apply. English doesn't allow suggest + person + to-infinitive, or suggested to wait. Say He suggested (that) I apply / should apply or He suggested applying.2

Formal vs everyday: suggest that she read (no -s) is the formal subjunctive; everyday British English often uses should or a normal verb instead. Apologise is the usual British spelling; American English uses apologize.12

Listen for it

Real-world task · next chapter or episode

In the novel you're reading, find three reporting verbs in a row of dialogue or narration (she insisted, he denied, they urged). For each one, note the pattern that follows. On screen, listen for a character retelling a row: "And then he accused me of…!" Bring the best one back to the chat.
