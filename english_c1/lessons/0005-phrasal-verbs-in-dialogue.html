Lesson 5 · Vocabulary

Phrasal verbs in dialogue

The verbs that carry the drama in every row, reunion and wind-up on screen.

Time ~3 min Win put the pronoun in the right place Mission follow who's annoyed and who's been let down

The big idea

People rarely say tolerate or disappoint; they say put up with and let someone down. Learn each verb as a whole,2 then learn where the object goes.1

Relationships

fall out with · make up · let someone down

You promised. Don't let me down. (disappoint me)3

Tolerating and annoying

put up with · wind someone up · get away with

I don't know why she puts up with him. (accepts something unpleasant)4

Ideas, topics and impressions

come up with · bring up · pick up on · come across as

She's come up with a plan. (thought of)5

Where does the object go?

pronoun in the middle · preposition keeps it together

bring it up (never bring up it) · put up with it1
