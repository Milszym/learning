Past tense, present meaning

Why a native speaker says "It's time we went" about right now.

Time ~3 min Win decode the unreal past after 4 triggers Mission catch what characters wish, regret and resent

The big idea

After some phrases, a past form means not real, not past time: unreal now → past simple, unreal past → past perfect.1

wish / if only

+ past simple · past perfect · would

If only we'd known.

It's (high) time

+ subject + past simple

It's high time we went home.

would rather

+ other subject + past simple

I'd rather you didn't tell Mum.2

as if / as though

+ past for an unreal comparison

He talks to me as if I were five.3
