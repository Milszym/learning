Cleopatra and the Moon

"Ancient Egypt" feels like one moment. It lasted three thousand years.

Time 2 min

Guess firstCloser to Cleopatra: the Great Pyramid or the Moon landing?

Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the building of the Great Pyramid.

Why it feels wrong: "Ancient Egypt" lasted 3,000 years.4 The pyramid is near the start (c. 2560 BC)1; Cleopatra is at the very end (d. 30 BC).2

Retell it · out loud, once

"Cleopatra is closer to the Moon landing than to the pyramids: 2,500 years back, 2,000 forward.3 The pyramids were older to her than she is to us."

contemporarynoun

Someone living at the same time as someone else.5 Cleopatra and Caesar were contemporaries.
