# Mission: Curiosities

## Why
Collect surprising, true facts from every field and actually keep them: remember them months later and retell them well in conversation. Enjoy a daily dose of wonder, broaden general knowledge, and get rich C1 English reading along the way.

## Success looks like
- Retell any past curiosity in about 30 seconds, key numbers included, without looking.
- Explain *why* it's true (the mechanism or the reason it feels wrong), not just the headline.
- Spot a dubious "fun fact" and know how to check it.
- Pick up C1 words and phrases from each curiosity and use them.

## Constraints
- One curiosity = one lesson, 2–3 minutes max.
- All domains: science, nature, history, people, tech, mind, language. Rotate them.
- Quick browser quizzes with instant feedback; single-file HTML via build.py.
- Native language Polish, English nearly C1 (see ../english_c1).

## Out of scope (for now)
- Deep courses on any single field. Go deep only when a curiosity sparks a follow-up question.
