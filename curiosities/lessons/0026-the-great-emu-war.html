The Great Emu War

In 1932 an army went to war against birds. The birds won.

Time ~2 min Win the best "the army lost to whom?" story Bonus 1 C1 word

Guess firstIn 1932 Australia sent soldiers with machine guns against wild emus eating farmers' wheat. How did it go? Decide before you scroll.

The army pulled out twice, and the emus kept eating the wheat.

~20,000emus in the Western Australian wheatbelt

~10bullets per claimed kill

Why it feels wrong: Machine guns should beat birds. But the emus split into small groups, ran fast and soaked up bullets.1 Major Meredith and two gunners gave up after a week in November 1932, came back, and quit in December.2 He claimed 986 kills for 9,860 rounds, a figure historians suspect was inflated.1 Bounties and fences later did far more.

Retell it · say it out loud once
  1. Hook: "Did you know Australia once sent the army to fight emus, and lost?"
  2. Numbers: "1932: three soldiers, two machine guns, about 20,000 emus. Roughly ten bullets for every bird they claimed."
  3. Kicker: "One MP said any medals should go to the emus: they had 'won every round so far'."2

fiasconoun

Something planned that goes wrong and is a complete failure, usually in an embarrassing way.3 The Emu War was a national fiasco: the birds simply outran the bullets.
