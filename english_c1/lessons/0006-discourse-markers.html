Lesson 6 · Vocabulary

Discourse markers

The little words that tell you where a conversation is about to turn.

Time ~12 min Win tell conceding, contrasting, correcting and changing topic apart Mission hear the "but" coming before the character says it

The big idea

Discourse markers such as anyway, I mean and mind you organise speech and show the speaker's attitude.1 Their dictionary meaning is often less important than their job, so the useful question is: what is the speaker doing with the next sentence? Catch the marker and you know whether a criticism is about to be softened, a mistake corrected or a topic dropped.

Four jobs

Contrasting with yourself

mind you · having said that · then again · still

He's untidy. Mind you, I'm not much better. (UK: makes what you said less strong)2

I hate musicals. Having said that, I loved Hamilton. (despite what I just said)3

I'd love a dog. Then again, who'd walk it? (a new, opposite thought)4 · It rained all week. Still, we had fun. (nevertheless)5

Conceding

to be fair · fair enough

The food was slow. To be fair, they were short-staffed. (admitting a point on the other side)6

"I can't, it's Mum's birthday." "Oh, fair enough." (I understand your reason; UK informal)7

Correcting and revealing

actually · I mean · as it happens

"You're from Warsaw?" "Kraków, actually." (a polite correction or a surprising fact)8

It's Friday. I mean Saturday. (correcting yourself or adding detail)9

"Know a plumber?" "As it happens, my brother is one." (by chance, not planned)10

Changing the topic

anyway

Anyway, enough about me. How's the new job? (moving on)

Anyway, as I was saying… (back to the main topic) · Anyway, thanks for calling. (wrapping up)11

Formal cousins: in writing, contrast is more often built inside one sentence with although, despite, whereas or while.12 In speech, the markers above usually start a new sentence.

Polish trap

Actually is not aktualnie. It means "in fact" or "really", never "currently". Aktualnie pracuje w Londynie is She's currently working in London, not Actually she is working in London.8

Listen for it

Real-world task · next episode or podcast

Chat podcasts are perfect for this. Each time you hear anyway, to be fair, mind you or actually, ask: conceding, contrasting, correcting or changing the topic? Count how often anyway drags a rambling story back on track. Bring one tricky example back to the chat.
