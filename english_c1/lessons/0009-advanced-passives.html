Advanced passives

From the newsreader's "He is believed to have fled" to the pub's "He got sacked".

Time ~3 min Win decode 4 passive patterns in news and chat Mission follow crime dramas and gossip

The big idea

The passive focuses on what happened, not who did it.2 News uses the reporting passive; casual speech uses get.1

It is said / thought / believed that…

It + be + past participle + that-clause

It's thought that the fire was started deliberately.1

Someone is said to (have)…

be said/thought/believed + to do · to have done

The suspect is believed to have fled the country.1

The get-passive

get + past participle · informal, often bad news

We got stuck in traffic.4

have / get something done

have/get + object + past participle

I'm getting my hair cut on Saturday.3
