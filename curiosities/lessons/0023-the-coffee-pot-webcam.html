The coffee pot webcam

The web's first famous live picture answered one question: is there any coffee left?

Time ~2 min Win a geeky origin story for any tech chat Bonus 1 C1 word

Guess firstThe camera usually called the world's first webcam was pointed at what? Decide before you scroll.

It watched a coffee pot in a Cambridge University computer lab, so people upstairs could avoid a wasted trip to an empty pot.

Why it works: In 1991 Quentin Stafford-Fraser and Paul Jardetzky pointed a camera at the pot by the Trojan Room, so colleagues on other floors could check it from their desks: a greyscale picture, updated about three times a minute.1 In November 1993 it went on the World Wide Web and became a landmark of the early web. Switched off in 2001, the pot sold on eBay for £3,350.2
