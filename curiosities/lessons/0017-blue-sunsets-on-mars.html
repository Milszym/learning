Blue sunsets on Mars

On the Red Planet, the colours of the sky are flipped.

Time ~2 min Win a sky-colour fact you can actually explain Bonus 1 C1 word

Guess firstWhat colour is the glow around the setting Sun on Mars: deep red, pale blue, or dull grey? Decide before you scroll.

On Mars, the sky around the setting Sun glows blue, while the rest of the sky turns yellow to orange.

RedEarth's sunset: air molecules scatter the blue away
vs
BlueMars's sunset: fine dust sends blue forward

Why it feels wrong: Mars's air is thin, about 0.6% of Earth's surface pressure, so fine dust does most of the scattering.2 Those dust grains are just the right size to scatter blue light forward, so it stays close to the Sun, while red and yellow spread across the whole sky.1 NASA's Curiosity rover photographed one in colour on 15 April 2015. Only the patch near the Sun is blue.

  2. Detail: "In 2015 NASA's Curiosity rover photographed a Martian sunset: a cold blue glow around the Sun, a yellow-orange sky everywhere else."
