Certainty & necessity

StructurePatternOn screen
Past deductionmust / might / can't have + pp"You must have known." · "She can't have left." (not mustn't have)
Unnecessary past actionneedn't have + pp
didn't need to + inf		"I needn't have cooked" = I cooked; it was pointless.
"I didn't need to cook" = no necessity; maybe I didn't.

Inversion & emphasis

StructurePatternOn screen
Inverted conditionalsShould / Were / Had + subject…"Should you need anything…" · "Had I known, I'd never have come."
Negative adverbial inversionLittle / Never / No sooner + aux + subject"Little did he know…" · "No sooner had we sat down than…"
Cleft sentencesWhat X is… / It was X that…"What I can't stand is the lying." · "It was you who told her!"
Emphatic auxiliarydo / does / did (stressed) + inf"I did lock it!" · "She does care, you know."

StructurePatternOn screen

+ past perfect (past)
