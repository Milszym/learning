Lesson 10 · Vocabulary

Idioms in conversation

Idioms characters use when they're fed up, dodging the truth, or wrapping things up.

Time ~3 min Win decode everyday spoken idioms Mission follow arguments and office drama

The big idea

Learn idioms by what the speaker is doing (losing patience, avoiding a topic, judging timing), not one by one. That's also how you'll guess a new one from context.

Losing patience

the last straw · the final straw

His lying was the last straw. (the latest problem that makes it all unacceptable)1

Honesty and avoidance

beat about / around the bush

Stop beating about the bush. (avoiding the important point)1

Timing

call it a day

I'm shattered. Let's call it a day. (informal: stop for now)2

Attitude and judgement

a blessing in disguise

Losing that job was a blessing in disguise. (bad at first, good in the end)1

Practice

Five quick lines. The last one is review.
