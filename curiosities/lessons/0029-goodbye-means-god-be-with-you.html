Goodbye means "God be with you"

You say a blessing every day. It's just been worn down.

Time ~2 min Win the story hidden in an everyday word Bonus 1 C1 word

Guess first"Goodbye" started life as a longer phrase that got squashed. Which one? Decide before you scroll.

"Goodbye" is a squashed "God be with ye".

Why it works: Phrases we say constantly wear down. "God be with ye" (late 1300s) shrank through forms like "God be wy you" and "God b'uy" to "godbwye" in the 1570s.1 Then "God" became "good", because people matched it to other farewells like "good day" and "good night".12 By the 1590s it was "good-bye", and speakers no longer heard the blessing inside.

contractionnoun

A short form of a word or combination of words, often used instead of the full form in spoken English.3 "Goodbye" began as a contraction of "God be with ye", just as "don't" is one of "do not".
