Retell cards

#HookKey numbersThe why
1 Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the Great Pyramid. Pyramid c. 2560 BC · Cleopatra 69–30 BC · Apollo 11 1969. Gaps: ≈ 2,530 vs 1,998 years. Ancient Egypt lasted 3,000+ years; she was a Greek (Ptolemaic) queen at its very end. The pyramids were older to her than she is to us.
2On Venus, a day (one spin) is longer than a year, and it spins backwards.One spin ≈ 243 Earth days · one orbit ≈ 225 · sunrise to sunrise ≈ 117.Venus spins extremely slowly and the "wrong" way (retrograde), so the Sun rises in the west.
3An octopus has three hearts and blue blood.2 gill hearts + 1 body heart · copper (haemocyanin), not iron.Copper-based haemocyanin turns blue with oxygen and suits cold, oxygen-poor water. The body heart stops when it swims, so it prefers to crawl.
4About half of people counting basketball passes miss a gorilla walking through the game.Simons & Chabris 1999 · 46% of 192 viewers missed it · on screen 5 s.Inattentional blindness: attention is a spotlight, and what falls outside the task may never reach awareness.
5The famous moth in the Harvard logbook was not the origin of the word bug.Edison's "bugs" 1878 · moth in the Mark II 9 Sept 1947."First actual case of bug being found" is a joke: engineers already called faults bugs. Hopper popularised the story.
6"Nice" first meant foolish or ignorant.Latin nescius "not knowing" · "foolish" late 1200s · "pleasant" 1769.ne- ("not") + scire ("to know"). It drifted through "timid", "fussy" and "precise" before becoming a compliment.
7Oxford was teaching students before the Aztecs founded Tenochtitlan.Teaching by 1096 · Tenochtitlan 1325 · Aztec Empire 1428. Gap: 229 years.Oxford has no founding date, but even its growth after 1167 predates Tenochtitlan. The Mexica people arrived c. 1250; it's their capital and empire that are younger.
8Botanically, a banana is a berry and a strawberry is not.Berry = 1 flower, 1 ovary. A strawberry carries ~200 tiny fruits (achenes)."Berry" is a kitchen word older than botany. A strawberry's flesh is the swollen flower base; its "seeds" are the real fruits.
9Earth has more trees than the Milky Way has stars.~3 trillion trees (Crowther et al., 2015) vs 100–400 billion stars: roughly 8–30 trees per star.The new count calibrated satellite maps with 400,000+ ground plots; the old satellite-only guess was 400 billion.
10In a group of just 23 people, two probably share a birthday.23 people = 253 pairs = 50.7%. 70 people = 99.9%.It's any pair, not you: pairs grow much faster than people. Uneven real birthdays only raise the odds.
11Sharks are older than trees.Shark-like scales ~450 million years; oldest fossil forest ~390 million; shark teeth ~410 million.Early land plants were small and low. Even if the oldest scales aren't true sharks, the teeth still beat the trees.
12Woolly mammoths were still alive when the Great Pyramid was built.Pyramid c. 2560 BC; last mammoths on Wrangel Island c. 2000 BC (~4,000 years ago): ~500 years of overlap.Rising seas stranded a herd on an Arctic island off Siberia about 10,000 years ago; it lasted ~6,000 more years.
13You're taller in the morning than at bedtime.About 1–2 cm (average 19.3 mm, ~1% of height); over half lost in the first hour up.Spinal discs are squeezed by body weight all day and soak water back up overnight.
14Where does OK come from: a Choctaw word, a war code, or a joke?23 March 1839, Boston Morning Post: "o.k." = "oll korrect"; 1840 "Old Kinderhook" campaignAn 1830s craze for joke abbreviations; Van Buren's campaign made this one stick
15Your phone's map would be useless without Einstein+45 µs (gravity) − 7 µs (speed) = 38 µs a day ≈ 10 km of error a dayClocks tick faster in weaker gravity; GPS turns time into distance at light speed
16Why does sealed honey never go off?About 17–18% water (nectar 60–80%); pH about 3–4.5; a little hydrogen peroxideToo dry, acidic and sugary for microbes: they die of thirst; open jars can absorb water and ferment
17On the Red Planet, sunsets are blueCuriosity rover, 15 April 2015; Mars air is about 0.6% of Earth's pressureFine dust scatters blue light forward, near the Sun; red and yellow spread across the sky
18Would a sugar pill work if you knew it was a sugar pill?Kaptchuk 2010: 80 IBS patients, 3 weeks; 59% relief on open-label placebo vs 35% with no pillsThe ritual of taking pills helps by itself; small pilot, self-reported symptoms, not cured disease
19How short was Napoleon, really?"5 pieds 2 pouces" ≈ 1.69 m, not 1.57 m; typical French conscript about 1.65 mFrench inches were longer (27.07 mm); Gillray's "Little Boney" cartoons and his tall guards did the rest
20Old church windows are thicker at the bottom, but glass doesn't flow.Westminster Abbey glass (1268) flows at most ≈ 1 nm per billion years: ≈ 14 nm over the age of the universe.Old methods (the crown process) made uneven panes, and glaziers usually set the thick edge down. The sag was there on day one.
21Crows remember human faces and scold the ones they fear, for years.7 crows trapped in 2006 by people in a caveman mask · once 47 of 53 crows scolded it · stopped only in 2023, after ≈ 17 years.A neutral mask was ignored, so they read faces. Crows that never saw the trapping learned the grudge from others.
22"Robot" comes from Czech robota: the forced labour serfs owed their lords.Karel Čapek's play R.U.R. (1920) · in English by 1923 · robota abolished 1848.Čapek wanted "labori"; his brother Josef suggested "roboti". Polish robota, same root, just means "work". His robots were synthetic flesh, not metal.
23The camera usually called the first webcam watched a coffee pot in Cambridge.Camera 1991 (lab network) · on the web November 1993 · about 3 frames a minute · off in 2001; pot sold for £3,350.Researchers on other floors were tired of climbing the stairs to an empty pot, so they checked it from their desks.
24"Bacteria outnumber your cells 10 to 1" is a myth: it's roughly 1 to 1.≈ 38 trillion bacteria vs ≈ 30 trillion human cells (70 kg man, 2016) · ratio ≈ 1.3:1 · bacteria weigh ≈ 0.2 kg.The 10:1 came from a rough 1972 guess about gut volume. One trip to the toilet may tip the balance to human cells.
25Olympus Mons on Mars is about 2.5 Everests tall, with a base the size of Poland.≈ 22 km (21–26 km, depending on the reference) vs Everest 8.85 km · ≈ 600 km wide · ≈ 300,000 km² (Poland 312,700; Arizona 295,000).No moving plates: the crust sat over one hot spot and lava kept piling up. Gravity only about a third of Earth's. Slopes so gentle you wouldn't notice the climb.
26In 1932 Australia sent soldiers with machine guns against emus, and the emus won.~20,000 emus vs 3 soldiers, 2 Lewis guns · claimed 986 kills for 9,860 rounds (≈ 10 per bird, probably inflated).The emus split into small groups and ran; the army withdrew twice. Bounties and fences later worked better.
27Three doors, one car: after the host shows a goat, switching wins twice as often.Stick 1/3 · switch 2/3 · ~10,000 letters to Parade (1990), nearly 1,000 from PhDs.Your first pick is right 1 time in 3; the host knows and never reveals the car, so the other door carries 2/3. Only if he always opens a goat door.
28Dried tardigrades survived 10 days in open space and revived with water.FOTON-M3, Sept 2007 · 10 days · 258–281 km up · few survived full solar UV.Vacuum kills by drying; they went up already dried and dormant ("tun"). UV, not vacuum, was the killer.
29"Goodbye" is a worn-down "God be with ye".Phrase late 1300s · "godbwye" 1570s · "good-bye" 1590s.Frequent phrases shrink; "God" became "good" by analogy with "good day", "good night".
30Voyager 1's signal takes almost a full day to reach Earth.Launched 1977 · ~61,000 km/h · one light-day (25.9 bn km) on 18 Nov 2026 · ~23.6 h in mid-2026.Radio travels at light speed, no faster; a command and its reply now take about two days.
