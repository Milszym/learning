# Mission: English, from nearly-C1 to effortless media

## Why
Enjoy English-language films, series, podcasts and novels in full: no subtitles, no re-winding, no nuance slipping past. That is the CEFR C1 listening benchmark: "understand television programmes and films without too much effort" ([Council of Europe](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/cefr-descriptors)).

## Success looks like
- Watch an episode of a dialogue-heavy series (e.g. a British comedy-drama) with no subtitles and follow jokes, sarcasm and implied meaning.
- Hear advanced grammar in fast speech (reduced forms like *must've*, *shoulda*; inversion; cleft sentences) and know exactly what it means.
- Recognise and understand the idioms, phrasal verbs and collocations native speakers use casually.
- Read a contemporary novel without looking up a word every page.

## Constraints
- About 15 minutes a day.
- Native language: Polish (expect trouble with articles, aspect and prepositions).
- Self-assessed as "nearly C1": skip B1–B2 basics unless the diagnostic shows a gap.
- Preferred format: quick browser quizzes and real-world input (clips, articles).

## Out of scope (for now)
- Exam preparation (CAE/IELTS) and exam-specific tasks.
- Business or academic writing.
- Pronunciation or accent training for speaking.
