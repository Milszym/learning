Napoleon was not short

A unit mix-up and a British cartoonist shrank an emperor.

Time ~2 min Win a myth you can debunk with numbers Bonus 1 C1 word

Guess firstWas Napoleon short for his day, average for his day, or tall for his day? Decide before you scroll.

Napoleon was about 1.69 m (5 ft 6½ in): roughly average, or a little above, for a Frenchman of his time.

Why it feels wrong: French records gave his height as about "5 pieds 2 pouces", but a French pouce (inch) was 2.71 cm, not 2.54, so English readers heard "5 foot 2".2 British cartoonists, above all James Gillray, drew him as tiny "Little Boney", and his tall Guard grenadiers made him look small beside them.1 Estimates range from 1.67 to 1.69 m.3

Retell it · say it out loud once
  1. Hook: "How short was Napoleon, really?"
  2. Numbers: "His records say '5 foot 2', but in French inches, which were longer: about 1.69 m. The average French soldier was around 1.65."
  3. Kicker: "Napoleon wasn't short. He was shrunk by a unit conversion and a British cartoonist."

smearnoun

An attempt to harm someone's reputation by publicly accusing them of something unpleasant.4 "Little Boney" began as a British smear, and it has outlived Napoleon by two centuries.
