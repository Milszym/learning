Lesson 12 · Review

Mixed review

Sixteen lines from Lessons 2–11, all shuffled together. No new rules, just retrieval.

Time ~12 min Win find what has stuck, and what hasn't yet Mission recall it at the speed of real dialogue

Why mix it up?

In a single-topic lesson you know which rule is coming, so the question does half the work for you. On screen, nobody warns you that an inverted conditional or a reporting verb is next. A large review of learning research rated two techniques as the most effective: practice testing (pulling answers out of memory) and distributed practice (spacing reviews out over time).1 This lesson does both. It also mixes topics, so each question first makes you work out which rule applies.

How to take it Don't look back at the lessons first. Getting stuck is part of it: the effort of retrieving is what makes it stick. Each missed area in your summary links back to its lesson.
