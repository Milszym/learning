Three hearts, blue blood

The octopus runs on copper, not iron, and it needs a spare heart or two.

An octopus has three hearts and blue blood, coloured by copper instead of iron.

3hearts: two for the gills, one for the body
Cucopper, not iron, carries its oxygen

Why it works: two small hearts push blood through the gills; the third sends it round the body.1 Oxygen rides on haemocyanin, a copper protein that turns blue when it picks oxygen up (and is colourless without it).2 It works well in cold, oxygen-poor water. The catch: the body heart stops while the octopus swims, so it prefers to crawl.3
