Bananas are berries

The fruit with "berry" in its name is the one that isn't.

Time ~2 min Win a fruit fact that starts arguments Bonus 1 C1 word

Guess firstTo a botanist, which one is a true berry: a strawberry or a banana? Decide before you scroll.

Botanically, a banana is a berry and a strawberry is not.

1flower with one ovary makes a banana: a true berry
vs
~200tiny fruits (the "seeds") on one strawberry

Why it feels wrong: "Berry" is a kitchen word, far older than botany. For botanists, a berry is a fleshy fruit grown from one flower with a single ovary: bananas, grapes, tomatoes, blueberries.14 A strawberry's red flesh is the swollen receptacle, the base of the flower. Each "seed" on the outside is an achene, a tiny dry fruit with its own seed inside.23

Retell it · say it out loud once
  1. Hook: "Is a banana a berry? And is a strawberry?"
  2. Detail: "A berry grows from one flower with one ovary. A strawberry's flesh is the swollen flower base, dotted with about 200 tiny fruits."
  3. Kicker: "Those 'seeds' on a strawberry are its real fruits. The berry is the banana."

misnomernoun

A name that does not suit what it refers to, or the use of such a name.5 "Strawberry" is a botanical misnomer: it isn't a berry at all.
