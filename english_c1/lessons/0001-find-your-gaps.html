Why start here

The Council of Europe describes C1 listening like this: you can follow speech "even when it is not clearly structured and when relationships are only implied," and understand "television programmes and films without too much effort."1 A lot of that implied meaning sits in small grammar: can't have versus must have, an inverted Should you…, a stressed I did. Miss one and you miss the joke.

You rate yourself as nearly C1, so I don't want to re-teach what you already know. This quiz samples the British Council's C1 grammar syllabus2 plus the idioms and collocations native speakers drop into dialogue.

How to take it Answer from your gut. Don't look anything up and don't agonise; a wrong answer here is useful data, not failure. Read the explanation after every question, including the ones you get right.
