The invisible gorilla

You see far less than you think, especially when you're busy counting.

Time ~2 min Win a famous experiment you can explain in 30 seconds Bonus 1 C1 word

Guess firstPeople counting basketball passes in a video. A person in a gorilla suit walks right through the game. How many viewers miss it? Decide before you scroll.

In the 1999 study, about half the viewers never saw the gorilla.

46%of 192 viewers missed the surprise
5 sthe gorilla was on screen
50%missed it even when it stopped and beat its chest

Why it feels wrong: we believe we see everything in front of us, but attention works like a spotlight: what falls outside the task may never reach awareness. This is inattentional blindness.2 Rates varied: some viewers got a woman with an umbrella instead, and harder counting meant more misses. In a small follow-up, a gorilla that stopped and beat its chest was still missed by half.1

Retell it · say it out loud once
  1. Hook: "Would you notice a gorilla walking through a basketball game?"
  2. Numbers: "In 1999, Simons and Chabris asked people to count passes. About half missed a person in a gorilla suit, on screen for five seconds."
  3. Kicker: "You don't see with your eyes; you see with your attention."

obliviousadjective

Not aware of something, especially of what is happening around you.3 Busy counting passes, half the viewers were completely oblivious to the gorilla.
