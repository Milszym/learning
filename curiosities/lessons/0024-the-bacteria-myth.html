The bacteria myth

You've heard you're "90% bacteria". The real count is roughly a draw.

Time ~2 min Win a polite correction of a famous "fact" Bonus 1 C1 word

Guess firstHow many bacteria live in your body for every one of your own human cells? Decide before you scroll.

You carry roughly as many bacteria as human cells: about 1.3 to 1, not the famous 10 to 1.

Why it feels wrong: The 10:1 figure came from a 1972 back-of-the-envelope estimate that assumed a whole litre of gut packed with bacteria, and textbooks repeated it for decades.1 Sender, Fuchs and Milo recounted in 2016, with wide error bars. The ratio also swings: one trip to the toilet removes a quarter to a third of the colon's contents, which may briefly tip the balance in favour of human cells.12

Retell it · say it out loud once
  1. Hook: "Heard that you're mostly bacteria? Guess the real ratio."
  2. Numbers: "About 38 trillion bacteria vs 30 trillion human cells: roughly 1.3 to 1. And all the bacteria together weigh only about 0.2 kg."
  3. Kicker: "It's basically a draw, and every trip to the toilet shifts the score."
