The toughest animal

About half a millimetre long, and it has survived open space.

Time ~2 min Win the ultimate "tough animal" fact Bonus 1 C1 word

Guess firstIn 2007 dried-out tardigrades ("water bears") spent 10 days outside a spacecraft, in open space. What happened when they came home? Decide before you scroll.

Dried tardigrades survived 10 days in open space and came back to life with a drop of water.

10days exposed to space, September 2007
~270 kmorbit height (258–281 km)
UVthe real killer: few survived full sunlight

Why it works: Vacuum kills mainly by drying you out, and these tardigrades went up already dried, curled into a dormant "tun" with metabolism almost stopped. So vacuum and cosmic radiation alone caused no loss in survival.1 Unfiltered solar UV was different: under the full spectrum, only a handful recovered.2 They became the first animals known to survive open space.3

Retell it · say it out loud once
  1. Hook: "Which animal has been left outside a spacecraft for ten days and lived?"
  2. Numbers: "2007, ESA's FOTON-M3 mission: about 270 km up, 10 days. Vacuum alone killed none extra; raw sunlight killed most."
  3. Kicker: "They didn't fight space. They switched themselves off and waited for water."
