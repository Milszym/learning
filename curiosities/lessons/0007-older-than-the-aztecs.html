Older than the Aztecs

Oxford was already teaching students before the Aztec capital existed.

Time ~2 min Win a date clash that rearranges your mental timeline Bonus 1 C1 word

Guess firstWhich came first: teaching at Oxford, or the founding of Tenochtitlan, the Aztec capital? Decide before you scroll.

Teaching at Oxford existed by 1096, over two centuries before Tenochtitlan was founded in 1325.

Why it feels wrong: we file the Aztecs under "ancient" and Oxford under "Europe", so we never compare them. Oxford has no founding date, but teaching existed there in some form by 1096 and grew fast after 1167.1 Tenochtitlan's traditional founding date is 13252, and the Aztec Empire only formed in 1428.3 The caveat: the Mexica people themselves arrived in the Valley of Mexico around 1250.

Retell it · say it out loud once
  1. Hook: "Which is older: Oxford University or the Aztec capital?"
  2. Numbers: "Oxford was teaching by 1096. Tenochtitlan was founded in 1325, and the Aztec Empire came in 1428."
  3. Kicker: "When the Aztecs built their capital, Oxford had already been teaching for over 200 years."

Deserving respect because of age, high position, or religious or historical importance.4 Oxford is a venerable institution: it was teaching students before Tenochtitlan existed.
