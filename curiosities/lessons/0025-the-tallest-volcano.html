The tallest volcano

Mars has a volcano that would cover Poland and tower over Everest.

Time ~2 min Win a size comparison that sticks Bonus 1 C1 word

Guess firstHow much taller is Olympus Mons on Mars than Mount Everest (above sea level)? Decide before you scroll.

Olympus Mons rises about 22 km, roughly 2.5 times the height of Everest, and its base is about the size of Poland.34

Why it works: Mars has no moving tectonic plates, so its crust sat over the same hot spot and lava kept piling up in one place. On Earth, plates drag volcanoes off their hot spots, which is why Hawaii is a chain of islands. Mars's gravity is also only about a third of Earth's.2 Heights vary (21–26 km) because Mars has no sea level to measure from.1
