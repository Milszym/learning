A day longer than a year

On Venus, you'd have a birthday before the planet finished one turn.

Time ~2 min Win a planet fact that sounds impossible Bonus 1 C1 word

Guess firstWhich takes longer on Venus: one spin on its axis, or one trip around the Sun? Decide before you scroll.

Venus takes longer to spin once than to orbit the Sun, and it spins backwards.

243Earth days: one spin
vs
225Earth days: one orbit

Why it feels wrong: we assume every planet spins fast, like Earth. Venus turns once every 243 Earth days but circles the Sun in about 225.1 It also spins the "wrong" way, so the Sun rises in the west. One caveat: because of that backwards spin, sunrise to sunrise takes only about 117 Earth days,2 so "day" here means one full spin.

Retell it · say it out loud once
  1. Hook: "On which planet is a day longer than a year?"
  2. Numbers: "Venus takes 243 Earth days to spin once, but only 225 to go round the Sun. And it spins backwards."
  3. Kicker: "On Venus, the Sun rises in the west, and your birthday comes before the planet completes one turn."
