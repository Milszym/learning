Lesson 6 · Vocabulary

Discourse markers

The little words that tell you where a conversation is about to turn.

Time ~3 min Win tell conceding, contrasting, correcting and changing topic apart Mission hear the "but" coming before it's said

The big idea

Markers like anyway, I mean and mind you organise speech and show attitude.1 Ask: what is the speaker doing with the next sentence?

Contrasting with yourself

mind you · having said that · then again · still

He's untidy. Mind you, I'm not much better.2

Conceding

to be fair · fair enough

The food was slow. To be fair, they were short-staffed.3

Correcting and revealing

actually · I mean · as it happens

"You're from Warsaw?" "Kraków, actually."4

Changing the topic

anyway

Anyway, enough about me. How's the new job?5

Polish trap

Actually is not aktualnie: it means "in fact", never "currently".4
