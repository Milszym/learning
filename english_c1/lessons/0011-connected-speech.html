Lesson 11 · Listening

Whatcha gonna do?

Decode the squashed words of fast dialogue: gonna, dunno, didja and friends.

Time ~3 min Win turn reduced forms back into full English Mission follow fast dialogue without rewinding

The big idea

In fast speech, "the pronunciation of a word will change depending on the words around it."1 Your job is only to hear these forms and rebuild the full words, not to write them.

Weak forms (schwa)

unstressed to, of, a, you → /ə/

a piece of cake → /ə piːs ə keɪk/2

Elision (dropped sounds)

/t/ or /d/ between consonants disappears

I can't do it → "I can' do it"3

Assimilation (sounds merge)

/d/ + you → /dʒ/ · /t/ + you → /tʃ/

did you → "didja"4

gonna · dunno

going to (future only) · (I) don't know

I'm gonna ring her, never I'm gonna the pub.5
