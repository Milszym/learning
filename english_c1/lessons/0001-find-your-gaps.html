Why start here

At C1 you can understand "television programmes and films without too much effort,"1 and a lot of implied meaning sits in small grammar. This quiz samples the British Council's C1 grammar syllabus2 plus the idioms and collocations natives drop into dialogue. Answer from your gut: a wrong answer is useful data, not failure.
