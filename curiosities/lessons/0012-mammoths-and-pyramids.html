Mammoths and pyramids

The Ice Age and ancient Egypt overlapped by about five centuries.

Time ~2 min Win a time-overlap story that sounds made up Bonus 1 C1 word

Guess firstDid the last woolly mammoths die out before or after the Great Pyramid of Giza was built? Decide before you scroll.

The last woolly mammoths lived on Wrangel Island until about 4,000 years ago, centuries after the Great Pyramid was built.

Why it feels wrong: We file mammoths under "Ice Age" and pyramids under "history". Mainland mammoths had mostly vanished by about 10,000 years ago, but rising seas stranded a small herd on Wrangel Island, in the Arctic Ocean off Siberia.12 Its youngest remains are radiocarbon-dated to about 2000 BC,3 roughly 500 years after Khufu's pyramid (c. 2560 BC).4 That is the last dated bone, so the exact end is uncertain.

Retell it · say it out loud once
  1. Hook: "Were there still mammoths alive when the Great Pyramid was built?"
  2. Numbers: "Pyramid about 2560 BC. Last mammoths about 2000 BC, on Wrangel Island. An overlap of roughly 500 years."
  3. Kicker: "Not in Egypt, of course: they lingered on a lonely Arctic island off Siberia."

lingerverb

To take a long time to leave or disappear.5 Mammoths lingered on Wrangel Island for about 6,000 years after the sea cut it off.
