Lesson 4 · Grammar

Ellipsis in conversation

How "Seen it.", "Neither have I." and "I'd love to." say a whole sentence in two words.

Time ~3 min Win rebuild 4 kinds of short reply Mission follow fast, clipped dialogue

The big idea

Natives leave out anything the listener can recover (ellipsis) or swap a whole clause for a small word like so or not (substitution).1

So do I / Neither have I

so / neither / nor + auxiliary + subject

"She loved it." "So did I."23

I hope so / I'm afraid not

so = yes clause · not = negative clause

"Will it stay dry?" "I hope so." / "I hope not."4

I'd love to

keep "to", drop the rest

"Fancy a drink later?" "I'd love to."15

Sounds good. Want a lift?

drop the subject and/or auxiliary

[I've] Seen it. · [Do you] Want a lift?15

Polish trap

Mam nadzieję, że nie is I hope not, never I don't hope so.
