Taller in the morning

You shrink a little every day, and grow back every night.

Time ~2 min Win a body fact anyone can test with a tape measure Bonus 1 C1 word

Guess firstWhen are you tallest: just after waking up, or just before bed? Decide before you scroll.

You are roughly 1–2 cm taller when you wake up than when you go to bed.

~19 mmaverage daily swing in height (about 1%)
>50%of the day's loss happens in the first hour up
~70%regained in the first half of the night

Why it works: The discs between your vertebrae are water-rich cushions. Standing and sitting squeeze them all day, so they thin out; lying down lets them soak up water again.23 Two small studies both found an average of 19.3 mm, with individuals ranging from 8 to 26 mm.12 Measure later than dawn and you'll see less: one study found about 7 mm over seven hours.4

Retell it · say it out loud once
  1. Hook: "Did you know you're shorter now than when you woke up?"
  2. Numbers: "About 2 cm, around 1% of your height. Over half of it goes in the first hour after you get up."
  3. Kicker: "Your spinal discs are sponges: squeezed all day, refilled all night."

imperceptibleadjective

Unable to be noticed or felt because it is very slight.5 The daily shrinking is imperceptible in the mirror, but a careful measurement catches it.
