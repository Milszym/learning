Crows remember faces

Upset one crow, and a whole neighbourhood may remember you for years.

Time ~2 min Win a creepy-but-true animal story Bonus 1 C1 word

Guess firstIn 2006 a scientist in a caveman mask trapped seven crows. How long did the local crows keep scolding that mask? Decide before you scroll.

Seattle's crows scolded the mask for about 17 years, including crows that were never trapped.

7crows trapped in 2006 by people in the mask
47 of 53crows that scolded him on one masked walk
17 yearsuntil the scolding stopped (September 2023)

Why it works: John Marzluff's team at the University of Washington trapped and ringed crows while wearing a caveman mask; a "neutral" Dick Cheney mask was mostly ignored, so the birds were reading faces, not masks in general.13 Crows that had only watched, and chicks born later, learned the grudge from others.2 Scolding peaked in 2013, then faded.3

Retell it · say it out loud once
  1. Hook: "How long do you think a crow can hold a grudge against one human face?"
  2. Numbers: "Seven crows trapped in 2006 by a man in a caveman mask. Once, 47 of 53 crows scolded him. It stopped only in 2023."
  3. Kicker: "Crows that were never caught joined in: they taught each other who the villain was."

hold a grudgeidiom

To keep a strong feeling of anger and dislike for someone who you feel treated you badly, often for a long time.4 Seattle's crows held a grudge against the caveman mask for 17 years.
