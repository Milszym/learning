Intensifying collocations

Why natives say "bitterly disappointed" and "absolutely exhausted", and never "very exhausted".

Time ~3 min Win pick the right "very" word Mission hear how strongly a character really feels

The big idea

Each "very" word has favourite partners, and extreme adjectives don't normally take very.12

Feelings

bitterly disappointed · deeply regret

He was bitterly disappointed not to get the part.3

How likely, how sure

highly unlikely · strongly suspect · fully aware

It's highly unlikely he'll call back.4

Judging a situation

utterly ridiculous · painfully obvious · perfectly normal

It's perfectly normal to feel nervous.5

very vs absolutely

very + gradable · absolutely + extreme

very tired → absolutely exhausted12
