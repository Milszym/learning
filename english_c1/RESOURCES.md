# English (C1) Resources

## Knowledge

- [CEFR descriptors, Council of Europe](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/cefr-descriptors)
  The official definition of each level. Use for: judging what "C1" actually requires and grounding the mission.
- [British Council LearnEnglish: C1 grammar](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1)
  14 free C1 grammar pages with explanations and exercises (inversion, cleft sentences, ellipsis, participle clauses, unreal time, modals of probability, phrasal-verb word order...). Use for: the grammar syllabus and each lesson's primary reading.
- [Cambridge Dictionary: English Grammar Today](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/)
  Concise, authoritative grammar reference with real examples (e.g. [cleft sentences](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/cleft-sentences-it-was-in-june-we-got-married)). Use for: checking a rule and for citations.
- [English Vocabulary Profile (Cambridge / English Profile)](https://englishprofile.org/?menu=evp-online)
  Searchable CEFR level for words, phrasal verbs and idioms. Use for: choosing vocabulary that really is C1, not B2 or C2.
- [Oxford Learner's Dictionaries + Oxford Collocations Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/collocations/)
  Free learner dictionary and 250,000 collocations. Use for: word meanings, idioms, which verbs go with which nouns.
- [Book: _Practical English Usage_ by Michael Swan (OUP)](https://elt.oup.com/catalogue/items/global/grammar_vocabulary/practical_english_usage_4th_edition/)
  The standard reference on tricky usage problems for advanced learners. Use for: settling fine distinctions.
- [Tool: Language Reactor](https://www.languagereactor.com/)
  Browser extension for Netflix/YouTube: dual subtitles, click-to-translate, one-key rewind, auto-pause per line. Use for: mining real dialogue for lessons and "intensive listening" practice.
- [BBC Learning English](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/)
  Short high-quality audio and video (6 Minute English, The English We Speak). Use for: real-world input with transcripts.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/EnglishLearning](https://www.reddit.com/r/EnglishLearning/)
  Large learner subreddit where native speakers answer. Use for: "does this sound natural?" questions and nuance checks.
- [italki](https://www.italki.com/) / [Tandem](https://www.tandem.net/)
  Paid tutors or free language exchange. Use for: testing comprehension and idioms in live conversation (optional).
